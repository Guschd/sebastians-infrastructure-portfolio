# Architecture

This section documents the engineering methodology behind planning, implementing and operating enterprise IT infrastructure.

It is not intended to describe specific production environments or vendor-specific configurations. Instead, it focuses on the principles, decision-making processes and architectural concepts that guide successful infrastructure projects.

The documents reflect a practical engineering approach that balances technical requirements with operational, financial and organizational constraints.

## Objectives

- Develop sustainable infrastructure architectures
- Align technical solutions with business requirements
- Support maintainability and operational excellence
- Ensure transparent decision-making
- Promote standardization and documentation
- Enable long-term scalability

## Engineering Approach

Infrastructure projects are treated as engineering projects rather than isolated technical tasks.

Every solution begins with understanding the operational requirement, evaluating technical and organizational constraints, developing multiple implementation options and selecting the most appropriate solution together with all relevant stakeholders.
