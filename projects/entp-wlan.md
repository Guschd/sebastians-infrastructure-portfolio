# Ruckus Enterprise WLAN

## Overview

Deployment of a new enterprise WLAN infrastructure as part of the German Hospital Future Act (KHZG).

## Objective

Provide secure, reliable and scalable wireless connectivity for clinical and administrative environments.

## Responsibilities

- Technical project coordination
- VLAN implementation
- Switch migration
- Ruckus SmartZone configuration
- Commissioning
- Troubleshooting
- Coordination of external service providers

## Technologies

- Ruckus SmartZone
- Enterprise WLAN
- VLAN
- Managed Switches
- WatchGuard Firewall

## Outcome

Successful rollout of a modern enterprise wireless infrastructure with improved coverage, performance and maintainability.
